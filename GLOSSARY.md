# Memento

Memento is a personal support tool: an external structure for remembering and initiating
contact with people, spotting overload early, keeping a timeline of patterns and symptoms
for clinicians, and parking ideas so they do not have to be acted on immediately.

Single context. Terms below are project-specific; general programming concepts are
excluded even if used heavily.

## Language

### Core

**Entry**:
A raw capture — voice or text — stored exactly as captured, with no manual tagging or
structuring at capture time.
_Avoid_: note, memo, log entry, record

**Capture**:
The act of recording an Entry: one tap, speak 10–30 seconds, done; text input as
fallback.
_Avoid_: log, add, create

**Inbox** (reviewable inbox):
The set of automatically extracted suggestions (people, dates, mentions) awaiting the
user's review. Non-blocking: capture never waits for it.
_Avoid_: queue, pending items

**Nudge**:
A gentle prompt generated by a deterministic rule (for example, hours up several days
in a row). Never a score or a streak.
_Avoid_: alert, notification, warning

**Rule** (early-warning rule):
A deterministic condition over Check-ins and Entries that may fire a Nudge. Rules-based
first; no ML.
_Avoid_: model, detector, trigger

### People and contact

**Person**:
Someone the user wants to maintain contact with. Carries name, optional birthday,
last-contact date, and notes.
_Avoid_: contact, friend, client

**Mention**:
A link between an Entry and a Person — something said about that person in that entry.
Extracted automatically into the reviewable inbox.
_Avoid_: reference, tag

### Early warning

**Check-in**:
A quick daily data point: energy (1–5) and hours worked. Two per day, one tap each.
_Avoid_: mood log, daily log

**Energy**:
A 1–5 self-rating of current energy, captured as part of a Check-in.
_Avoid_: mood, score

### Parking lot

**Idea**:
A parked interest or idea with a creation date and a resurface date about a month later.
_Avoid_: thought, todo, task

**Parking lot**:
The collection of parked Ideas, resurfaced after about a month to reduce the urge to act
immediately.
_Avoid_: backlog, list

**Resurface**:
Bringing a parked Idea back to attention after its resurface date.
_Avoid_: remind, notify

### Review and clinical

**Weekly review**:
A one-page summary of a week — energy, sleep, social contact, silence tolerance, what
drained and what restored — generated as a single prompt over that week's entries.
_Avoid_: report, digest, summary

**Bedrijfsarts**:
The Dutch occupational physician who may receive an exported Weekly review or timeline.
A recipient, never an actor in the app.
_Avoid_: doctor, physician (when referring to this role)

**Clinician export** (deferred to v2):
An export of entries or reviews for a bedrijfsarts, assessment, or coach. Only the
user decides who sees it.
_Avoid_: share, report

**Crisis resources** (deferred to v2):
Local (Dutch) crisis or occupational-health contacts shown when a pattern looks like a
crisis. Never clinical interpretation, never auto-contact.
_Avoid_: diagnosis, triage
